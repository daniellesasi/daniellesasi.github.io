BAD HAIR DAY
A platform game built around a character whose hair changes form to provide different abilities. The propeller form allows the character to fly, the mop form clears obstacles and reveals hidden items, and the multi-leg form enables movement across walls and ceilings. Progression is based on switching between these abilities to navigate different environments.
This project was created using Blender, ComfyUI, Illustrator, and hand-drawn sketches.
LALALANDERS
A life-simulation game centred on character customisation, interior design and everyday activities. Players create and dress characters, furnish individual spaces and develop different rooms using modular furniture and decorative assets. The project includes character design, environment design, props and isometric room layouts.
This project was created using Blender, ComfyUI, Illustrator, and hand-drawn sketches.
ICY TOWER RETURENS
A vertical platform game that reimagines the tower as the world itself. The player begins beneath the Earth and climbs through a sequence of stacked environments, moving from subterranean caves into the ocean, woods, city, sky and space, before reaching an alien UFO world . Each layer introduces its own platforms, hazards and movement challenges, turning the climb into a continuous journey from the deepest point underground to the furthest edge of the universe.
This project was created using Blender, ComfyUI, Illustrator, and hand-drawn sketches.

HERO WORSHIP
This turn based combat game concept details the character design and animation centred around unconventional warfare. Offering a humanistic take on daily situations, the game replaces physical violence with mental debates and social power games. Utilising a distinct cel-shaded 2D aesthetic, the visual direction draws heavily from personal experience in the fashion industry, bringing functional styling to the garments. The result is a unique integration of high-fashion sensibilities with classic gaming aesthetics.
This was created in Blender, CLO3d and AfterEffect

This mobile runner developed as an educational tribute to human biology, this mobile runner prototype takes players on a visceral, miniaturized journey through the body. The visual direction draws conceptual inspiration from the classic animation Once Upon a Time... Life, combining 1980s airbrush aesthetics with a thorough study of microscopic organ anatomy. The introductory level, "The Tongue," introduces the core gameplay loop: players must navigate a textured, organic landscape, evading sharp hazards and gathering essential nutrients before transitioning into the throat sequence.
WELCOME TO THE MACHINE
This 3D puzzle adventure game concept utilizes a surrealist art direction to explore themes of institutional conformity and urban isolation. The narrative follows an anomalous protagonist attempting to break free from the oppressive, cyclical routines of a monolithic city system. Players must solve puzzles to navigate through the game. The concept art establishes a clear visual trajectory, transitioning from the vibrant, chaotic isolation of neon cityscapes into the overexposed, sterile, and fragmented architecture of the corporate sector.
This was created in UE5, Blender, CLO3d and Substance

CHARACTERS
This platformer designed for both mobile and console platforms. The narrative follows a stranded main character who is trapped in an alien universe where he cannot naturally survive. To endure this hostile environment, the protagonist must navigate the world from within the safety of a protective bubble. The core gameplay loop consists of platforming missions focused on exploring levels to collect scattered parts of his crashed ship. Players must carefully manage their traversal and survival mechanics to fully rebuild the vessel and return home.

PERFECT WHITE
This mobile arcade game is following a romantic story of the player looking for the perfect white gem. The core gameplay is a quest to collect rare gems to craft the perfect jewelry for their loved one. Players navigate dynamic levels, utilizing a unique mechanic where the character swallows the collected gems for safe transport while actively dodging enemies and environmental obstacles.
This was created in Illustrator and programmed with Claude Code

DANIELLE SASI

About

Gamer at heart and 3D Artist with over a decade of experience as a Creative Design Lead in the luxury fashion industry. After an accomplished career designing ready-to-wear, I have transitioned into the gaming space, applying my expertise in garment construction and creative concepts to virtual worlds.

My background includes serving as Creative Lead and Head of Design at PANGAIA, designing runway and commercial collections at JW Anderson, and co-founding the digital fashion brand PERSONA. Backed by formal coursework in Computer Science at Tel Aviv University and Unreal Engine training with Epic Games, I utilize Unreal Engine 5, Blender, Marvelous Designer, and CLO3D. I also integrate multiple AI platforms—composing custom models in ComfyUI for textures, modeling, and motion—to build highly detailed characters, garments, and interactive environments.
